# Roll & push (id: `roll`)

- **World:** Build (world id `build`), "Roll home!" (icon 🎢). Source: app/src/lessons/roll.js, roll-courses.js, roll-pieces.js, roll-sound.js; engine base app/src/engine/lesson.js.
- **Learning goal:** a baby hedgehog curls into a ball and rolls along a wooden toy track to Mummy. Something is in the way; she taps a yellow block, pushes a blue box or ramp, or presses a round button, and the physics does the rest. Level 3 makes her work out what comes first.
- **Levels:** 3. L1 "Tap" 10 puzzles, L2 "Push" 12 puzzles, L3 "Think" 12 puzzles (34 in all). One baby comes home per puzzle, so the nest ends with 10 / 12 / 12 babies.

## Engine behaviour that applies to every puzzle

- **Rounds:** a "round" is one puzzle. Each level is its own play session: the "voiced first time only" set, the "hand demo already shown" set and the baby collection order start fresh every level.
- **Voice rule in this game:** each line is spoken (if it has a clip) the FIRST time in a level; after that the same text is caption only (`sayLine`). The engine also never voices the same line twice within 30 s.
- **Go! badge:** this game replaces the engine rule. "Go!" flashes (1.3 s, twinkle) as soon as the table has risen and no hand demo is playing, which in the first puzzle is while the baby is still rolling out of the log. After a hand demo that is NOT on the puzzle's first stop, Go! flashes again when the demo ends.
- **She may act at any time**, not only when the baby has stopped: pieces work while it rolls. Tapping any piece while the two babies are peeking also picks a baby (below).
- **The stop (shared by every puzzle below, written STOP):** the baby brakes smoothly and stops 0.15 clear of the first closed thing. Its face goes calm (scared for 0.55 s at a hole, water or a long drop). It turns its head to the piece that helps for 1.8 s, then looks at the child for 1.4 s, repeating every 4 s. Mummy turns to watch it. The "Uh-oh" caption is shown, then straight away the instruction caption (see the bug note at the end). Hand demo (DEMO) 0.6 s later only if this kind of helper has not been demoed yet in this level.
- **DEMO:** a slow ghost hand. For a tap: one tap on the piece (up to 2.2 s). For a push: a drag from the piece along the track to its goal (up to 2.6 s). A halo and a 1.2 s bob on the piece. Input is locked 1.3 s; the demo state lasts 2.7 s. Any touch ends the hand.
- **Idle ladder (LADDER; resets on every touch; paused while the baby rolls, a demo plays, or a finger is dragging):** 6 s: spoken hint line (below) + the helper hops; 12 s: hint again + hop + 3 s halo + hand demo again; 20 s: errorless (the hand shows it, and 1 s later the helper does its job by itself, slowly); 45 s: "Bye bye! See you soon!" [voiced] then Home. Extra: stuck 8 s at a stop, the hand shows again, then every 14 s. Hint lines (voiced if they had clips, today caption only): yellow thing "Try the yellow one!", blue thing "Try the blue one!", button "Try the button!", jelly "Try the green one!". All [pending voice].
- **Wrong things:** nothing is ever really wrong. Tapping a yellow thing out of turn: it hops or wobbles and waits. A pushed piece that hits the end of its rail: it shakes "no" (and a ramp pushed to the rim of an unfilled hole also says "Fill the hole first!" [pending voice]). A pushable that is only tapped (not dragged): tap 1 shakes no, tap 2 replays the drag demo, tap 3 slides it one column toward its goal. Pressing the red decoy button: see 3-3. Hammering the screen (mash): Pip wobbles, scared face 0.7 s.
- **One position word per puzzle:** the first thing that happens in the puzzle that has a word ("Down it rolls!", "In it goes!", "Up the ramp!", etc.) is captioned; later events in the same puzzle say nothing. Each row names which one fires.
- **Success (landing home):** after the last obstacle the baby rolls to the lip of the chute (a held breath, 0.45 s, camera glides to the nest), plops down the chute (soft pup sound, joy face, squash), slides to the pad, rolls up the lane and along its row to its own cushion. Pip watches, nods or claps (joy face 1 s). The tune note for each of her actions has played as she acted (a rising little melody per level). Mummy turns, joy face, says the number ("One!", "Two!" ...) as it settles, and nuzzles / pats / sways (0.8 to 1 s). The count is always the puzzle number. The game waits up to 3 s for the settle, then 0.5 s of stillness. No confetti, no praise word.
- **Between puzzles:** while the baby is settling, the old table sinks into the plinth (right to left, 0.3 to 0.6 s per column), the pieces are swapped, and the new skyline rises left to right (0.35 to 0.65 s per column). Buttons slide out of their sockets after it is up. No pops, no fades. The table is ready in under 1.5 s.
- **The baby choice (every puzzle from the 2nd on, see the row B in each level):** two other babies peek from the log's two front windows.
- **Camera:** it frames the baby and everything she still needs (pieces and their goals), follows with a soft glide, never cuts, and is frozen while a finger is down. At the chute it swings to the nest and Mummy.
- **Mummy and Pip:** Mummy (hedgehog) sits on a mossy mound behind the nest and watches. Pip sits on a stump behind the log. Poking a nest baby makes it peek and wiggle with a soft note. Poking the rolling baby: it blinks, wiggles, looks at the helping piece.
- **Looks:** L1 birch wood, morning sky; L2 honey oak, midday sky; L3 cherry wood, golden late-afternoon sky, with two lanterns by the nest. Purple = lift, red/green = doors, yellow = tap, blue = push.
- **Persisting:** each baby that gets home is added to the child's kept collection (`rollHogs.count` and which of the 14 head-things (leaf, cherry, flower, acorn, bow, strawberry and so on) she has seen); babies she has not seen before come first. Every baby in a level looks different.

## Things that looked broken or odd (read from the code; nothing changed)

1. **Uh-oh line is overwritten at once.** At every stop the "Uh-oh!" caption and the instruction caption are issued in the same frame, so the instruction replaces the Uh-oh caption immediately and the child never reads it. Once the lines are voiced, the clip plays but the caption on screen will already be the instruction, so audio and caption disagree. (roll.js `onStop`.)
2. **3-4 "Door first" goes quiet after the door opens.** The first stop says "Open the door first!"; nothing says "push the box" afterwards until the 6 s idle hint "Try the blue one!" and the 8 s hand.
3. **3-8 "Crate or stone?" is not a choice.** The stone sits between her and the crate, so the stone always has to go first, and the only instruction is about the crate.
4. **Ramp gates say "Uh-oh! Too high!"** while the ramp itself is the thing in the baby's way and the step is farther on. Also the 2-1 and 2-4 box blocking the baby says "A block is in the way." and then tells her to push it into a hole she cannot yet see the need for; fine, but a more natural "Uh-oh! A box!" is absent.
5. **Intro lines collide.** "Let's roll home!" is cut after 0.6 s by "Help the baby roll home!" (both caption only today; the second interrupts the first). Same on every level.
6. **Baby choice words and timing.** "Which baby next?" is only shown the first time in a level, and the 2.6 s auto-pick is short for a 2-year-old; after puzzle 2 there is no word at all.
7. **Two-high block hint.** "Tap it again!" is said once per level (voiced once, caption only after), so the second two-high block (1-10) has only a caption.
8. **"Look what you made!" and "Everyone is home!"** have no voice and are said back to back in the finale ("Everyone is home!" first, then 5 s later the other); "Look what you made!" is not in tools/voice/new-lines.txt at all.
9. **Stale comments.** The top of roll.js says 10 / 12 / 12 puzzles and roll-courses.js says "the 34 puzzles"; the data has 10 / 14 / 14 (38). `makeCushions` still has an unused 12-puzzle branch.
10. **"Fill the hole first!"** can effectively never fire in the shipped puzzles (3-10's box is pushed in ahead of the ramp); harmless dead path.
