# Sink or float lab (id: `sink`)

- **World:** Beach (world id `beach`), "Sink or float?" (icon 🧪). Source: app/src/lessons/sink.js and sink-items.js; models in tools/blender/creatures/prop_sinklab.py.
- **Learning goal:** drops things in a water tank (with her own finger) to see whether they float or sink, then sorts each into the BLUE tub (floats) or the ORANGE tub (sinks); later guesses first and hears her guess said back, and tests which thing rolls and which bounces.
- **Levels:** 3, 6 rounds each. L1: drop, watch, sort. L2: guess, drop, watch, sort. L3: guess with surprising things (big, tiny), plus a "which one rolls?" and a "which one bounces?" test.
- Meta intro line: "Let's see what floats!" [voiced]

## Engine behaviour that applies to every round (engine/lesson.js)

- **Go! gate:** taps open as soon as the round is laid out; the Go! badge shows when the game is ready (forced after 3 s).
- **Hand demos (this game's own):** the first time EACH kind of action comes up (drag it into the water, guess, drag it into a tub, pick-roll, pick-bounce) a slow hand demo (up to 3.4 s) plays on the real thing; any touch ends it. Stuck for 8 s: it plays again, then every 14 s. The engine ladder's own 8 s hand is switched off for this game (`showHint = null`), so the two never stack.
- **Idle ladder (engine):** 5 s prompt again + nudge (the thing to use bobs; for a guess both signs hop); 8 s prompt again (voiced again) + nudge with a 3 s halo; 20 s errorless (the right tub only answers, halo on it; in a test the wrong thing goes dead); 90 s "Bye bye! See you soon!" [voiced] and Home.
- **Wrong answer** (`redirect`): input locks 0.7 s, the wrong tub turns a little "no" with one soft sound, the redirect line shows. 2nd wrong: errorless.
- **Voice:** a line is voiced only if its text is in assets/voice/index.json, at most once per 30 s (repeats are caption only). New lines are queued in tools/voice/new-lines.txt (marked [queued] below: caption only until voiced).
- **Success** (quiet): caption only, Pip (behind the tubs) watches with a joy face, Crab claps its claws (1 s, it carries on while the next round is laid out), 0.4 s look + 0.25 s stillness, then the next round (a test result: 0.55 s + 0.25 s).
- **Finale:** camera glides (1 s) to the two full tubs; Crab walks to the front between them and points at each; "Look what you made!" (voiced once per session) for 4 s while every thing in the tubs is pokeable (a press and a soft plop); photo (saved to the gallery); rain, 3-note resolve, "You did it! A sticker!" [voiced], sticker, Home.

## The lab (all levels)

- Left to right: a glass water tank (x -3), a wooden crate (the thing waits on its straw bed), a BLUE tub (x 3.4) with its sign behind it (blue board: a yellow rubber duck sitting ON the water) and an ORANGE tub (x 6.5) with its sign (orange board: a grey stone lying on the sandy floor under the water, bubbles rising). The blue tub has water in it; the orange tub has a high sandy bed (just under the rim): what she sorts stays there all level and shows over the rims. Pip watches from behind, between the two signs (x 4.8, z -2). Crab (half size) is the lab helper: it stands in front between the tank and the crate while things go in, and in front of the crate's right end while they are sorted (it scuttles between the two spots).
- **Camera stations** (eased glides, 0.8-0.9 s): TANK (tank + crate), TUBS (crate + both tubs and signs: the guess), LAB (tank + both tubs: pulling it out of the water into a tub; computed per screen from the camera's FOV and aspect so the tank and both tubs just fill the width: about 12.2 away at 1280x800, 11.1 at 1366x768 and 1920x1080), TEST (L3 slide / bounce on the sand), END (the two tubs). Any demo hand is cleared at every phase change and at every glide, and a new one only starts once the camera has arrived. Backdrop props that lined up with the lab (a lantern post seen through the tank, jellyfish behind the glass, the bonfire behind the orange sign) are hidden in this lesson, the sunset sun sets further left (it sat under the caption) and the night moon sits lower (it was cut by the top edge).
- **A new thing:** it pops onto the crate (0.45 s) while the camera glides to its station (0.85 s); she can use it as soon as it is on the crate (the crate is in view all through the glide); the prompt and any demo hand come when the camera has arrived.
- **Drop it in (the main action, a real drag):** she drags the thing off the crate. Over the tank it is held above the rim (never through the glass). While she holds it over the water, Crab clasps its claws to its face and screws its eyes shut, peeking now and then (the held breath). She lets go over the tank: it settles just over the rim at her finger's x (0.35 s) and drops in with real physics and a buoyancy force (one splash: small separate drops without ink, ripples on the surface). Let go anywhere else: it walks back to the crate (0.5 s, no sound). **Tap fallback:** a tap on the thing (or on the tank) carries it over on a slow, low arc (0.95 s, it stays well under the caption), a 0.3 s held breath, and lets go.
- **Watch (she can play):** while it settles (floater 1.7 s; sinker 1.0 s after it lands on the floor; at most 2.4 s) a tap on it (or the tank) pushes it under; a floater pops back up.
- **What happened:** no arrows; the thing bobbing on top or lying on the floor is the result. Crab claps (joy). L1: the result and the sort are one line (below). L2/L3: the result line, then the sort line (queued behind it). The camera widens to LAB (0.9 s) and she can pull it out at once; a tap on it still pushes it under. While it waits in the water to be sorted it glows softly (a halo that follows it), because it is behind tinted glass; the water itself is lighter than before.
- **Sort (a real drag):** she drags it out of the water (it comes out dripping, a small splash) straight to a tub and lets go over it. Right tub: it arcs in (0.45-0.8 s) and lands with one soft plop on the water (blue tub; it bobs a little there for the rest of the game) or the sand (orange tub), the tub squashes, the blue tub's water rings out. It STAYS. Wrong tub: that tub turns "no", the redirect line, and the thing goes back into the water at a calm pace. Let go anywhere else: back into the water. **Tap fallback:** tapping a tub or its sign sorts it (it hops out of the water, dripping, and arcs in). In the tubs, things lie side by side (front first); a thing that can't fit beside the others rests on top of a flat one (a leaf) rather than an apple. A leaf shown tilted on the crate lies flat in the tub.
- Tapping a sign or tub while it waits to be dropped: the sign wiggles (nothing wrong).
