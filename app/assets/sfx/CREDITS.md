Attribution is not required, but given here with thanks. Files were trimmed, converted to mono Ogg Vorbis, normalized,
and in a few cases pitch-shifted or low-passed for this app. Freesound items use the public HQ preview of the CC0 original.
`amb_*` files are 30 s seamless loops (3 s equal-power crossfade baked in), quiet (peaks <= -16 dBFS).
